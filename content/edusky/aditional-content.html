About the Journal

Edusky: Journal of Multidisciplinary Education Sciences is an international peer-reviewed journal that focuses on multidisciplinary scope throughout the field of educational sciences. The journal aims to be a platform that connects diverse perspectives, theories, and practices in education, facilitating cross-disciplinary discussions, and promoting deeper understanding of complex issues in education. This journal is published 3 times a year (March, July & November).
